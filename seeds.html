About Bounty Seed

A Seed Brand Built On Excel Crop Group's Standards

Bounty Seed was established to bring the same discipline Excel Crop Group applies to its insecticides, fungicides and fertilizers into the seed business. Every variety we offer, from hybrid corn and millet to basmati rice, maize fodder, sesame and mustard, is selected and handled with germination and field performance in mind.

Our seed lots move through sourcing, cleaning, grading and packing under close supervision, so what a farmer opens at sowing time is what was promised on the pack, in both quality and quantity.

Research & Development

Selecting Varieties For Pakistan's Fields

Every variety carrying the Bounty Seed name is evaluated with local growing conditions in mind, soil types, sowing windows and the pressures of heat, water availability and pests that Pakistani farmers deal with season after season.

We track how each variety performs across regions before it is offered widely, and we keep listening to feedback from dealers and farmers once it is in the field, feeding that experience back into how future seed lots are selected and prepared.
