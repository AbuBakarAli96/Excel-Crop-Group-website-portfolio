Our Commitment

Quality Control, Research & Development

Behind every product on our shelf is a quality control process supported by our own research and development effort. We maintain testing labs where incoming raw material and finished formulations are checked before a product is approved for sale, so that what reaches a farmer's field matches what is printed on the label.

Each product in our range goes through this testing process as a standard step, not an exception. Our research team continues to study formulation chemistry after a product has already launched, looking for ways to improve stability, shelf life and field performance, so the products farmers rely on this season keep getting better in the seasons that follow.

We also treat customer feedback from the field as part of this research loop. Reports from dealers and farmers about how a product performs in real conditions feed directly back into how we review and refine our formulations.

Our Expertise

Manufacturing, Formulation & Packing

Excel Crop Group operates its own fertilizer production line alongside dedicated SL (Soluble Liquid) & EC (Emulsifiable Concentrate) plants, giving us direct control over how our soluble liquid and emulsifiable concentrate products are formulated, mixed and finished. This in house capability lets us manage consistency from batch to batch rather than depending entirely on outside processors.

Our packing plants follow international standard practices for measuring, sealing and labelling, so that every sachet, bottle, can and bag leaves the facility with accurate fill volumes and secure packaging. The process is carried out under the supervision of experienced staff, using formulation types matched to each product's active ingredients and intended field use.

Our Story & Valuesہماری کہانی اور اصول

Our Story

Excel Crop Group began with a simple goal, to bring genuine, effective agricultural inputs to farmers without the confusion of counterfeit or unregistered products crowding the market. Working out of our Multan facility, we built a product range that now covers six major categories of crop care, from seed to harvest. What started as a small operation has grown steadily, one trusted order and one repeat customer at a time, always guided by the same principle we started with: sell only what we would use on our own fields.

What We Offer

Our portfolio spans insecticides, fungicides and herbicides for crop protection, fertilizers and micronutrients for plant nutrition, and quality seeds to start the season right, a complete range designed around how Pakistani farms actually operate. Every category is chosen to solve a real problem farmers face in the field, not to fill a catalog, and our team can walk a customer through which product fits their crop, their soil and their budget.

Our People

Behind every order is a team that understands local growing conditions. From warehouse staff checking batch numbers to field agronomists answering questions directly over the phone, our people are the reason customers keep coming back. Many of our team members grew up in farming families themselves, which means the advice we give comes from lived experience, not just a product manual.

Community & Support

Beyond selling products, our team offers free crop doctor guidance. Farmers can call or email our team with details of an affected crop and receive practical advice from people who understand local growing conditions. We also spend time in the field with dealers and farmers directly, listening to what is and is not working, because the best product improvements come from conversations, not assumptions.

Looking Ahead

As we grow, our focus remains the same, expand our product range thoughtfully, stay close to the farmers we serve, and continue building the kind of reputation that comes only from consistent, genuine service. We are investing in our formulation and testing capability so that every new product we introduce meets the same standard as the ones farmers already trust us for.
